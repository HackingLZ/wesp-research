Identity

One driver, several security planes

WESP is a Rust-based kernel policy platform with a C minifilter bridge.

Security review

No kernel exploit confirmed; three client defects are actionable

High impact

Any reachable Rust panic deliberately bugchecks the machine with tag RUST.

Client DoS

Disconnecting a queue inside its callback self-deadlocks and reaches a five-minute fail-fast.

Client DoS

Registration scans two caller UTF-16 pointers without a length or maximum.

Delivery

A failed IOCP post only logs, potentially stranding an in-flight notification and payload.

Hardening

User buffers are probed and copied; enum, alignment, length, overflow, capability, rundown, and transaction checks are pervasive.

Best material targets: cross-client authorization, nested rule parsing, and queue cancellation races.
